Elite Technical Training Institute in Laikipia

Elite Technical Training Institute is a technical and vocational training institution located in Wiyumiririe, Laikipia County, Kenya. We provide practical skills and career-focused training in beauty and hair, hospitality, baking, computing, information technology, photography and media.

Our goal is to equip learners with practical technical skills that can help them build careers, start businesses and become self-reliant.
